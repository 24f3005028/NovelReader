# Design direction

Reference review: [WebNovel's official app screenshots](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qidian.Int.reader), [WebNovel on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/webnovel-read-novels-manga/id1234939196), and [Royal Road's catalogue](https://www.royalroad.com/home).

These references informed the discovery hierarchy: compact app header and search, featured fiction, category chips, cover-led shelves, small metadata, and persistent library navigation. This project uses original branding and artwork rather than copying another app's assets.

The requested palette is near-black `#05070B` with neon blue `#168BFF`. Raised surfaces use restrained blue-black tones; borders provide separation without turning every card into a glowing box. Blue indicates active navigation and primary actions. White and muted blue-grey carry reading hierarchy. Reader paper/sepia/custom colours remain optional for comfort.

The logo is an original luminous open spellbook with a star above the spine. It is used in the app header, launcher, favicon, and splash assets. Three original sample-fiction covers have dedicated fantasy illustrations. Imported novels use their real source cover URLs, with a title fallback when an image is unavailable.

Discovery has a featured story and three-column cover grid. The library prioritizes reading position; a persistent continue-reading strip keeps the last story within reach. Reader chrome is minimal, with typography controls available from Aa. No invented star ratings, rankings, readership counts, or download figures are shown.
